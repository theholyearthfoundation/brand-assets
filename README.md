## 🏷️ Who owns the marks, and what may be done

**The Holy Earth Foundation**, its **golden heart** and its **builders' mark** (the three birds) belong to The Holy Earth Foundation. No registration is claimed in these files.

- **You may** use the name factually, to say who THEF is or what a thing came from.
- **Members and press** may use the marks as [`BRAND-GUIDELINES.md`](BRAND-GUIDELINES.md) describes.
- **Everything else needs permission first:** merchandise, partnerships, commercial use, modified
  versions. Ask through [github.com/theholyearthfoundation](https://github.com/theholyearthfoundation).

## 🎨 Palette

"The canopy at first light", from [`arrival/org.css`](https://github.com/theholyearthfoundation/theholyearthfoundation.github.io/blob/main/arrival/org.css). The two vine strands are sky (A Course in Miracles, the Eagle) and gold (plant medicine, the Condor); where they meet is glow (single-mindedness, the Barn Owl).

| Colour | Canopy (dark) | Morning mist (light) |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0f2420` | `#edf5f1` |
| Text | `#eaf1e4` | `#12312a` |
| Sky | `#8ed1f2` | `#0b6a96` |
| Gold (the accent) | `#e9b949` | `#8f600a` |
| Glow | `#ffe3a1` | `#b07d12` |
| Leaf | `#86c98f` | `#2e7a44` |
| River | `#48b5a8` | `#13786c` |

**Type on the portal:** Literata for reading, PapyrusStd for headings and the brand only (a licensed face, credited on the pages that use it; font files are not kept here).

**Proposals** wear the fleet's amber family (`d97706`, `6b4f1d`, `b45309`, `fef3c7`, see [TONES](https://github.com/drasticstatic/my-template/blob/main/proposals-template/TONES.md)). That is a filing colour, not a brand colour.

These are the colours of the websites. Confirm the logo's own colours against a master file.
