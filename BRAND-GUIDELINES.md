# 📘 The Holy Earth Foundation Brand Guidelines

*Draft for the foundation's stewards to read and change. Plain-language guidance, not legal advice.*

## The marks

- **The Holy Earth Foundation:** the name. **THEF** is its short form.
- **The golden heart:** a heart in a circle of light, with vine leaves. The foundation's mark, the one it shows the world.
- **The builders' mark:** the Eagle, the Condor and the Owl in a golden halo. It marks the places where the work is made, and it never stands in front of the heart.

Use the full name on the first prominent use in a piece. Running text after that can be shorter.

## ✅ Please do

- Use the official files from this repo, unmodified, once they are here. Until then, use the files
  listed in the [README](README.md).
- Leave clear space around a mark, at least one eighth of its width on every side.
- Link back to [iamoneself.com](https://www.iamoneself.com) when you share a mark online.
- Describe the foundation accurately: it bridges A Course in Miracles and plant medicine traditions in a teaching called Plants & Miracles.

## 🚫 Please don't

- Stretch, recolor, rotate, add effects, or redraw the marks.
- Put the builders' mark in front of the golden heart, or use it where the foundation is speaking to the world.
- Combine the marks with another organization's logo in a way that implies endorsement or affiliation.
- Use the marks to suggest THEF endorses a product, substance, provider, retreat or event.
- Sell merchandise bearing the marks without written permission from The Holy Earth Foundation.

## Who may use the marks

- **The foundation's stewards, members and helpers:** yes, for the foundation's work.
- **Press and educators:** yes, to refer to THEF accurately.
- **Anything else** (merch, partnerships, commercial use): ask first.
